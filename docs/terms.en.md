## 3. LAN and the local service

- The local service uses unencrypted HTTP and the Sub-Store backend has no login. With LAN access on, devices on the same network can download subscriptions; with "LAN can open the management UI" on, they can view and modify your data. Only enable these on trusted networks and turn them off afterwards.
- Subscription links contain node credentials; keep them safe and do not publish them.
